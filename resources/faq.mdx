Whether you're evaluating Eloqaura for yourself or your organization, this section answers the questions that come up most often. Browse the topics below to understand who our programs are for, how they work, and what you can expect from the experience.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who are Eloqaura's programs designed for?">
    Eloqaura's programs are designed for leaders at every stage — from emerging managers stepping into their first leadership role to C-suite executives refining their strategic communication. Our team workshops are also built for corporate teams that want to strengthen how they listen, collaborate, and navigate conflict together.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I choose the right program?">
    The right starting point depends on your goals:

    - **Public Speaking Mastery** — best if you want to develop executive presence, command a room, or sharpen your storytelling. The Presence Amplifier and Storytelling Blueprint modules are available within this track.
    - **Team Communication Workshops** — best for teams working through communication breakdowns, conflict, or collaboration challenges. Workshops include the Listening Lab and Conflict to Collaboration.
    - **Executive Communication Coaching** — best if you want one-on-one, ongoing guidance tailored to your specific leadership communication needs.

    If you're unsure which track fits your situation, [schedule a consultation](/resources/contact) and the Eloqaura team will help you find the right fit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can programs be customized for my organization?">
    Yes. Eloqaura works directly with organizations to tailor program content to your industry, team dynamics, and communication objectives. Whether you need to adjust the focus of a workshop or combine elements from multiple tracks, the team can design an experience that fits your context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long are the programs?">
    Program length varies by format:

    - **Executive Communication Coaching** is an ongoing engagement, typically structured around your pace and goals.
    - **Workshops** (such as Listening Lab and Conflict to Collaboration) are typically delivered over one to two days.
    - **Multi-week cohort programs** are available for organizations that want a more sustained learning experience.

    Specific timelines are confirmed during your initial consultation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are programs delivered in person or virtually?">
    Both formats are available. Eloqaura delivers programs in person and virtually, depending on the specific program and your organization's needs. Format options are discussed and confirmed during your consultation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">
    Reach out via the [Eloqaura contact page](https://www.eloqaura.com/contact) to schedule an initial consultation. During that conversation, you'll share your goals and the team will recommend the right program or engagement path for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer corporate or group pricing?">
    Yes. Eloqaura offers pricing for organizations and groups. Contact the team directly to discuss your headcount, format preferences, and organizational goals so they can provide a proposal tailored to your partnership.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What results can I expect?">
    Outcomes vary by program and individual, but participants typically leave with:

    - Stronger **executive presence** and confidence in high-stakes communication
    - Greater **team cohesion** and reduced friction in collaborative work
    - Enhanced **storytelling** skills that make ideas land with impact
    - More **strategic communication confidence** across leadership contexts

    Eloqaura's philosophy is rooted in the psychology of connection — the goal is to transform your mindset around communication, not just add surface-level skills.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
