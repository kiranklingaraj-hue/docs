Eloqaura works with individuals seeking personal growth and with organizations bringing communication training to their teams. Whatever your starting point, the path forward begins with a conversation.

## Ways to engage

**Individual enrollment** — Join an open-enrollment program such as Public Speaking Mastery or Executive Communication Coaching. These are designed for professionals who want focused, high-impact development on their own timeline.

**Corporate and team partnerships** — Bring Eloqaura into your organization. Team workshops like the Listening Lab series can be tailored to your department's specific challenges—whether that's cross-functional collaboration, conflict resolution, or building a culture of clearer communication.

## The engagement process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore the programs">
    Browse the Programs section to understand what each offering covers and which fits your goals. If you're unsure, the [Executive Communication Coaching](/programs/executive-coaching) and [Team Workshops](/programs/team-workshops) pages include detailed descriptions of outcomes and formats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a consultation">
    Reach out through the contact page to schedule an initial conversation with the Eloqaura team. This is a no-commitment call to discuss your situation, ask questions, and identify the right program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin your program">
    Once you've chosen a path, the Eloqaura team will walk you through onboarding—whether that's enrolling in a scheduled cohort, booking individual coaching sessions, or coordinating a workshop for your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The initial consultation is the most valuable step you can take. Come prepared to describe a specific communication challenge—a high-stakes presentation, a team conflict, an upcoming difficult conversation. The more concrete your example, the more useful the conversation will be.
</Tip>
